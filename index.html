AreaStatusNotes

ColorPASSHigh-contrast mode available throughout
NavigationPASSKeyboard accessible, consistent structure
Touch TargetsPASSLarge interactive areas (44px minimum)
VoicePASSVoice alternative to typing throughout
MotionPASSReduced-motion mode and OS preference respected
LanguagePASSPlain language; English and Tamil support
Screen ReaderNEEDS REVIEWManual testing with real assistive tech required
FormsPASSClear labels and feedback on every input
